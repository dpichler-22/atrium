# Changelog

All notable changes to Atrium are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-19

The first public release.

### Features

- Large clock and date, three world times and EUR exchange rates.
- One search bar with Google suggestions, open tabs, your links and your history.
- Slash commands: `/claude`, `/gpt`, `/gemini`, `/yt`, `/x`, `/in`, `/drive`, `/img`, `/meetnow`, `/tab`, `/history` and `/ports`.
- Links in tabs and folders, with inline editing and live drag and drop.
- Google account switcher for Google links.
- Dark theme by default, with a light mode.
- English interface. Dates and suggestions follow your browser's language.
